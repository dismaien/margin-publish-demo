Cardamom Buns

Makes 12. A synthetic recipe for demonstration.

Dough

IngredientAmount
Flour500 g
Milk, warm250 ml
Butter, soft75 g
Sugar60 g
Yeast7 g
Ground cardamom2 tsp

Method

  1. Mix everything into a soft dough and knead for 8 minutes.
  2. Rise until doubled, about an hour.
  3. Roll out, spread with cardamom butter, fold, cut and twist.
  4. Bake at 200 °C for 12–14 minutes.
Timing

The second rise takes about 30 minutes in a warm kitchen.
